{# ======================================================== HIGH SCHOOL #}
High School · Grade 7 – 12

Dedicated tuition and an all-round education

The UCS High School has {{ stats.high_school_learners }} learners from Grades 7 to 12. Under the management of Deputy Principal and Academic Head, Mr Daniel Mathew, learners receive dedicated tuition and an all-round education.

The small classes have a ratio of {{ stats.class_ratio }}. We offer a comprehensive selection of subjects up to Grade 12 level, including mathematics, science and computer skills.

We have a {{ stats.matric_pass_rate }} matric pass rate which has been unbroken for several years.

We follow the CAPS curriculum. In Grade 10 learners choose their subjects and continue with them until they matriculate in Grade 12.
