How the import works
  • A learner is matched on the login e-mail, else the ID / birth certificate number, else name + date of birth — re-importing updates, it never duplicates.
  • Blank cells keep what is already recorded.
  • No learner e-mail? A login like name.surname.1@{{ generated_domain }} is generated and listed in the report.
  • Learners are enrolled in their grade and class; Grade 10 – 12 subject choices are checked against the CAPS rules.
  • Subjects stay locked until fees are paid, unless Fees paid up to (month) is filled in.
  • Each row is saved on its own: a row with errors is skipped and the rest still import.
