Your school calendar, in your own app

Add this address to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar and your sessions, tests, deadlines and reminders appear there automatically — and keep updating as they change.

Treat this like a password — anyone who has it can read your whole schedule.
How do I add it?

Google Calendar — Other calendars → + → From URL → paste → Add calendar.

Outlook / Microsoft 365 — Add calendar → Subscribe from web → paste → Import.

Apple Calendar — File → New Calendar Subscription → paste.

Where do I find my calendar's address?

Google Calendar — Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format.

Outlook / Microsoft 365 — Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → choose Can view all details → copy the ICS link.

Apple iCloud — right-click the calendar → Share Calendar → Public Calendar → copy the link.
