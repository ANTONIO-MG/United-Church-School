Terms & conditions

  • The application, once accepted by the registrar, forms the contract between you and UCS for the duration of your child's schooling.
  • Fees are paid in advance on or before the 1st of each month; arrears of one calendar month are taken as forfeiture of your child's place, and outstanding accounts are handed to the school's attorneys / debt collectors at your cost.
  • One full month's notice is given of any fee increase. One full term's notice is required when a learner leaves; no transfer card is issued before it is completed.
  • UCS takes all reasonable precautions for your child's safety; you indemnify the school against claims arising from injury, accident or loss, at school, on excursions or in transit, and authorise emergency medical treatment at your expense.
  • UCS will not tolerate verbal, physical, sexual, emotional or mental abuse of staff by any parent, learner or representative.

Codes of conduct & prospectus

  • No cell phones, earphones or speakers at school — they are confiscated until the end of the term. Random drug tests may be conducted (R90, parent's account).
  • Blazers and ties are worn at all times; correct school uniform, hair and nails as set out in the prospectus. Learners not in proper uniform are sent home.
  • Absence of ten days or more removes a learner from the enrolment register (Department of Education regulations); a doctor's note is required after two consecutive days.
  • Extra-murals run on Thursdays 13h30 – 15h00, on or off the premises; a learner not participating is dismissed at 13h30.
